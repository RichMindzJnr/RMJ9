About

The mind behind Design By RMJ

Design By RMJ is a creative design studio built on one belief: bold ideas deserve elegant execution. I help brands, businesses and individuals stand out through logo design, poster design, social media graphics and advertising visuals — crafted with intention, not just made to fill space.

Every project starts with listening. Whether you’re launching something new or refreshing how you’re seen, I turn your message into visuals that feel confident, clean and unmistakably yours.

Bold ideas, elegant designs. That’s not just my slogan — it’s how I work.
